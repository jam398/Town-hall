## About This Event

Join us for a beginner-friendly introduction to ChatGPT, the AI assistant that's changing how we work and learn. No technical background required!

## What You'll Learn

- Understanding what ChatGPT is and how it works
- Writing effective prompts to get better results
- Practical applications for work and personal use
- Tips for fact-checking AI responses
- Ethical considerations when using AI

## Who Should Attend

- Complete beginners curious about AI
- Professionals looking to boost productivity
- Educators exploring AI tools
- Anyone who wants to understand the AI revolution

## What to Bring

- A laptop or smartphone
- Curiosity and questions!
- Optional: A free ChatGPT account (we'll help you set one up)

## Instructor

**Dr. Sarah Chen** is Town Hall's Education Lead and former university professor. She specializes in making complex technology accessible to all learners.
